## Profile

Plant disease epidemiologist working at the interface of quantitative methods, data science and disease management. My research develops and applies phytopathometry, statistical and simulation modelling, and research synthesis (meta-analysis) to understand epidemics and support evidence-based decisions in tropical and subtropical cropping systems, with long-standing programmes on Fusarium head blight, wheat blast and soybean diseases. I am committed to open, reproducible science and to training the next generation of quantitative plant pathologists in Brazil and abroad.
